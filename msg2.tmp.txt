fix(catalogue): NEW tag for the two new products; re-frame the Fearless cutout

Two problems with the Laverne Fearless addition.

1. No NEW badge. The product carried `"added": ""`, which seo-gen reads as "no
   addition date", so neither the NEW badge nor the Newest First sort picked it
   up. Assaf Wild Colt Tobacco had the same gap for the same reason. Both are
   stamped 2026-10-29 now. Checked in Chromium: both cards carry
   `.pc-badge-new` reading NEW, while Wild Colt (2026-10-21, past the 7-day
   window) correctly has none.

2. The bottle image was badly framed. The cutout itself was sound but I had kept
   the source's 969x1000 canvas, in which Laverne's product photography places
   the bottle 49.5px left of centre and 27px below it. Under
   `.pc-img{object-fit:contain}` that shows up as a visibly off-centre bottle
   with lopsided margins, unlike the other cutouts (assaf-wild-colt-tobacco and
   laverne-king-tobacco are both dead centre).

   Rebuilt with a better matte and explicit framing: background colour is now
   the median of the 1px border rather than assumed pure white, the background
   mask is a border flood fill at a 14-level tolerance, alpha ramps over 22
   levels of deviation from that background, and enclosed near-background pixels
   (labels, highlights) are forced opaque instead of being punched through.
   The bottle is then tight-cropped and re-centred on a square canvas at 78% of
   the height, matching the repo convention.

   Result: 1033x1033 canvas, bottle 411x806, centre offset -0.5px, symmetric
   311px side padding. The 1024 webp and 500w srcset siblings were regenerated
   from it in the same pass, so the cards and the detail page agree.
