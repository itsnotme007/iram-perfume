fix(catalogue): split Wild Colt and Wild Colt Tobacco into separate products

Wild Colt and Wild Colt Tobacco are two distinct Assaf fragrances. An earlier
rename (3a655ae) collapsed them into one entry, which left the catalogue in a
state where the only bottle photo on disk belonged to Wild Colt Tobacco but was
being served as the Wild Colt image.

Split them again:

* Restore the tobacco photo to its own slug. assaf-wild-colt-tobacco.* is the
  cutout that shipped with the original Wild Colt Tobacco listing (768x768 RGBA)
  and it goes back to serving that product only.

* Add Assaf|Wild Colt Tobacco to products.json with the same price ladder as
  Wild Colt (3ml 99 / 5 155 / 7.5 229 / 10 285 / 20 529 / 30 729) but its own
  identity: Fragrantica WILD-COLT-TOBACCO-119905 plus the Parfumo
  wild-colt-tobacco page, and its own inspiration (Santal Royal Guerlain, the
  value this product carried before the rename). No added date, so it claims no
  NEW badge and no New Arrivals slot.

* Source a real Wild Colt bottle image from the Parfumo listing
  (parfumo.com/Perfumes/assaf/wild-colt, canonical p-main-img, 480x480) and turn
  it into a transparent RGBA cutout: background flood-filled from the border so
  white label areas stay opaque, alpha derived from the source's own
  anti-aliasing, and edge pixels un-composited off the white backdrop.

  Result is a 480x480 canvas with the bottle at ~21% of the width, matching the
  repo convention (assaf-wild-colt-tobacco.png is 768x768 at ~24%) and keeping
  the rendered img in line with the 554px site median rather than becoming a
  102px outlier. Transparency means it sits correctly on both the light
  (#FAF9F7) and dark (#1a1a2e) themes; measured contrast leaves 5.7% of bottle
  pixels near the light background and 2.1% near the dark one.

Also regenerates the OG card for Wild Colt, which did not exist while the
product was merged.

Catalogue is now 190 products.
