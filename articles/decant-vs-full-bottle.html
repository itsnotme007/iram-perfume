When a decant wins

Trying a new signature or an expensive niche profile — testing a fragrance for a full month through all weathers — building a 10-20 bottle rotation without a shelf of 100ml bottles.

When a full bottle wins

Your everyday ‘daily driver’ you finish bottles of — gifting and special editions that hold value — scents you have worn for years.
